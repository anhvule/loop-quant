Method. Market-factor (beta) model — drift anchored to beta × market drift (a stock's own momentum is refused as noise), volatility-standardised block bootstrap sampled jointly with SPY so correlations and co-crashes survive, with every percentile auto-checked against the stock's own realised history.

Data. SPY benchmark · Yahoo Finance daily closes · analysis runs locally, nothing leaves your machine except price requests. Calibration testing (90% bands covered ~90% of outcomes over 28 years of SPY) applies to the market model; single stocks with short histories are flagged as unvalidated. No output here is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything.
